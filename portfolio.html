Music Crafting Workflow

Utilizing Digital Audio Workstations (FL Studio, BandLab, and custom-developed DAWs) in synergy with generative audio tools like Suno AI. Every track is built on hands-on creation of rhythm, original motifs, and core melodies—ensuring unique structural complexity rather than simple automated prompts. Artwork and visuals are designed with Gemini, Canva, and Shotcut.

Short Movies & Video Workflow

Short films and videos are generated using Gemini Chat and Google Flow / Vids. The process begins with detailed storyboarding and narrative decomposition into individual rendered clip segments. These elements are then multi-track stitched, sound-engineered, and polished with video editors using custom mixes, audio overlays, and precision cut edits.
