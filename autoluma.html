Why AutoLuma? (In Simple Terms)

AutoLuma is a truly one-of-a-kind extension. Most video tools in web stores only give you manual sliders or hotkeys. That means when you settle back on your couch to watch a stream, you're forced to keep getting up or clicking around to manually crank up the brightness whenever a scene gets too dark, only to have to dial it right back down when daytime returns.

This is especially frustrating on platforms like YouTube, where video lighting varies wildly from creator to creator depending on their cameras and filming setups. Some videos are naturally bright, while others are muddy and unwatchable.

AutoLuma completely eliminates that hassle. It works quietly in the background, analyzing playback in real time without putting any load on your CPU (near 0% system usage). It calculates the optimal brightness boost automatically, bringing out the best in your monitor and revealing shadow details that would otherwise remain hidden in the dark.

Technical Highlights

  • ITU-R BT.709 Luma Sampling: Measures perceived luminance rather than basic RGB averages.
  • Strict 100% Floor: Never dims clean, well-lit videos or slide decks below their default baseline.
  • Statistical 8-Sample Lock: Analyzes playback trends, locks the final boost level, and shuts down to maintain zero CPU overhead.

Installation Instructions

  1. Download and extract autoluma.zip.
  2. In Chrome or any Chromium browser, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Enable Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.
