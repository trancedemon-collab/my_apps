UltraEQ

A minimal, ultra-lightweight 5-band global equalizer for Android. Eliminate boxy mids, restore kick presence, and match any earphones to your ears.

Download APK (Android) Direct download • Zero ads • Background service

Zero Bloat

No advertisements, no accounts, and no tracking analytics. Instant startup with zero impact on battery life.

Universal Control

Runs as a persistent background engine, shaping audio output system-wide across streaming, games, and video.

Hardware Neutralizer

Tames muddy, boxy midranges and smooths harsh treble typical of factory earphone tunings.

5 Precision Tuning Bands

  • 60 Hz Sub-Bass & Kick Depth
  • 230 Hz Bass Body & Warmth
  • 910 Hz Midrange Resonance & Vocals
  • 3.6 kHz Upper Midrange Clarity
  • 14 kHz Treble Shimmer & Air

Routing on Android 14+

  1. VLC Media Player: Settings > Audio > Audio output > Select AudioTrack (Java).
  2. Spotify: Settings > Turn ON Device Broadcast Status > Tap Equalizer to link UltraEQ.
  3. Global System Audio: To route audio sessions globally across all applications, connect to a PC once and execute:
    adb shell pm grant com.ultraeq.app android.permission.DUMP
