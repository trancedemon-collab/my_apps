UltraEQ

A minimal, ultra-lightweight 5-band global equalizer for Android. Correct muddy frequencies and take full control of your earphone audio profile.

Download APK (Android) Direct download • No telemetry • No ads

Zero Bloat

No advertisements, no accounts, and no background analytics. Runs fast and respects your battery.

Universal Control

Intercepts device audio sessions to reshape tone across your media, browsers, and streaming apps.

Hardware Neutralizer

Fixes muddy midranges and harsh treble inherent to budget and unbalanced earphones.

5 Precision Tuning Bands

  • 60 Hz Sub-Bass & Kick Depth
  • 230 Hz Bass Body & Warmth
  • 910 Hz Midrange Resonance & Vocals
  • 3.6 kHz Upper Midrange Clarity
  • 14 kHz Treble Shimmer & Air

Routing on Android 14+

  1. VLC Media Player: Settings > Audio > Audio output > Select AudioTrack (Java).
  2. Spotify: Settings > Turn ON Device Broadcast Status > Tap Equalizer to attach UltraEQ.
  3. Global System Audio: To capture all playback sessions globally, run this one-time command via ADB:
    adb shell pm grant com.ultraeq.app android.permission.DUMP
